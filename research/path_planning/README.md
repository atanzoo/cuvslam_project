# Path-planning research records

Use this directory for D1 Edu, RPLIDAR/Nav2 simulation, PPO, IMM, MPPI,
obstacle-avoidance, and navigation research notes. Executable tools and robot
models remain under `simulation/path_planning/`; generated outputs remain in
their declared output/data directories.

The dated handoffs in this directory are historical research records. They do
not prove D1 Max hardware behavior or real cuVSLAM/Nav2 integration.
